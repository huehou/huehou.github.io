## Vector Norm
The norm of a vector [^1] is defined through [[Inner Product|inner product]]
$$
\norm{f} = \sqrt{\langle f, f \rangle} \,.
$$
We can use this norm to normalise a vector to have unit norm
$$
\hat{f} = \frac{f}{\norm{f}} \,.
$$ 
## Distance between Vectors
The distance between vectors [^1] are defined as 
$$
D {\pqty{f - g}} = \norm{f -g} \,.
$$
Alternatively, we can use the Bures distance [^1]
$$
D_B {\pqty{f, g}} = \arccos {\pqty{\norm{\langle f, g \rangle}}} \,. 
$$



[^1]: Kevin Cahill (2019). Physical Mathematics (Second Edition). Cambridge University Press. Chapter 1.6, 13.
