The three Pauli matrices are defined as 
$$
\begin{align*}
\sigma_x ={}& \pmatrix{0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0} \,,\\
\sigma_y ={}& \pmatrix{0 & -\I \\ \I & 0} \,,\\
\sigma_z ={}& \pmatrix{1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1} \,.
\end{align*}
$$
