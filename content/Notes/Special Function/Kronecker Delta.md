By definition, [^1] 
$$
\delta_{ij} = \begin{cases}
0 \qif i \neq j\\
1 \qif i = j
\end{cases} \,.
$$
## Properties
### Connection to Dot Product
$$
\vb{a} \cdot \vb{b} = \sum_{ij} a_i \delta_{ij} b_j = \sum_i a_i b_i  \,.
$$ 
[^1]: Kevin Cahill (2019). Physical Mathematics (Second Edition). Cambridge University Press. Chapter 1.3, 5.
